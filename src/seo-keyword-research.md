# SEO keyword research notes

Updated: 2026-04-29

## Goal

Find high-intent SEO topics for `llmcfo.com` that match the current service:

- managed AI FinOps
- LLM cost optimization
- usage tracking
- billing reconciliation
- provider routing, caching, and batching

## What already exists on the site

Strong coverage already exists for:

- `llm cost optimization`
- `semantic caching`
- `model routing`
- `prompt caching`
- `provider arbitrage`
- `batch api routing`

Those are useful operational keywords, but the site has lighter coverage on broader category and commercial-intent searches.

## Highest-value gaps

### 1. AI FinOps

Why it matters:

- strong category fit for the service itself
- aligns with FinOps.org's active "FinOps for AI" framing
- closer to buyer / operator language than pure prompt-engineering terms

Recommended page:

- `/research/ai-finops`

### 2. LLM cost monitoring

Why it matters:

- strong operational pain-point keyword
- likely to attract teams already feeling spend pressure
- fits productized service language around tracking, reconciliation, and chargeback

Recommended page:

- `/research/llm-cost-monitoring`

Related supporting phrases:

- `llm cost tracking`
- `llm usage tracking`
- `ai cost observability`

### 3. OpenAI cost optimization

Why it matters:

- provider-specific intent with commercial urgency
- strong overlap with the existing service promise
- useful entry page for teams not yet thinking in multi-provider or FinOps terms

Recommended page:

- `/research/openai-cost-optimization`

## Secondary opportunities

- `Anthropic cost optimization`
- `AI cost observability`
- `LLM usage tracking`
- `OpenAI cost monitoring`
- `FinOps for AI`

## Notes on selection

- I prioritized terms that are both commercially relevant and close to the service offer.
- I did not prioritize broad low-intent AI education terms.
- I also avoided creating duplicate pages for topics the site already covers well.
