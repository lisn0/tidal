§ 02 · Techniques

Six levers we pull, every engagement.

Most engagements combine three or four of these. The audit tells us which dominate your spend surface.
01
30–50%
model routing

Right model, right request.

Classify each request and route to the cheapest model that passes your quality bar. Reasoning goes to flagships; extraction and classification go to small fast models.

02
20–40%
semantic cache

Near-duplicates, served instantly.

Fingerprint prompts with embeddings and serve identical answers from sub-10ms cache. Similarity thresholds and invalidation tuned per feature.

03
15–30%
prompt compression

Tokens that don't earn their keep.

Audit system prompts for redundancy. Deduplicate examples. Compress retrieved context with LLMLingua-style techniques. Every change A/B tested.

04
10–50%
batch & async

Non-interactive work pays less.

Route background jobs to batch endpoints (up to 50% discount) with SLO-aware queueing for anything time-sensitive.

05
20–35%
provider arbitrage

Same capability, different price.

Identical tasks often cost 2–3× more at one provider. We route by capability-per-dollar, not by the SDK your team happened to start with.

06
5–15%
fallback chains

Graceful degradation, not over-provisioning.

Smart retries and tiered fallbacks beat worst-case over-provisioning. Maintain SLO without paying flagship prices on every call.

§ 05 · FAQ

Common questions.

Missing something? Write to hello@llmcfo.com — we respond within one business day.

What does LLM CFO do? +
LLM CFO is the managed AI cost optimization platform. We audit LLM spend across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Bedrock, implement model routing, semantic caching, and prompt compression, and deliver monthly reconciled savings reports that tie back to raw provider invoices.
How much can we save? +
Median reduction across 84 engagements: 52% in the first full billing cycle after implementation. Range: 38–68%, depending on architecture.
How is pricing structured? +
Performance pricing. Free audit, then 15–25% of verified monthly savings vs. a locked baseline. No savings in a given month, no fee.
What's the minimum monthly spend? +
$20K+/month on LLM APIs, or teams projected to cross that within a quarter. Below that, self-serve tooling is a better fit.
Which AI providers are supported? +
OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini & Vertex, Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Groq, Together, Mistral, Cohere, Fireworks, and most OSS endpoints. Multi-provider setups typically have the largest savings surface.
How are savings verified? +
We lock a pre-engagement baseline from provider invoices. Every month the reconciliation reports compare optimized spend to that baseline, traffic-adjusted, reconciled to raw billing. Your CFO and board can audit the math.
Are you SOC 2 compliant? +
Yes. SOC 2 Type II certified. Read-only billing data by default; prompt data accessed only on explicit approval for specific optimizations.
Will optimization hurt output quality? +
No. Every change is A/B tested against production baseline for seven days minimum. Regressions roll back automatically. Quality is monitored continuously alongside cost.
