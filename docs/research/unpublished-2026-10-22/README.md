# Unpublished research and locale draft archive

These files were moved out of `src/` during review so Eleventy cannot publish
them. They are retained as drafts and are not approved content.

The provider-plan articles make precise usage-limit, per-seat, API-equivalence,
and product-comparison claims that are not supported by current primary sources
and in several places describe superseded product models or plans. The localized
research indexes link to those drafts and to translated article URLs that do not
exist. The model-deprecation translation also repeats broad contract and
accounting assertions from the English source without supporting references.
Resolve those evidence gaps and review the translations before moving any source
back under `src/`.

The PHP emergency patch article is intentionally not archived: its technical
claims link to the PHP advisories and official NSA/NCSC guidance.
